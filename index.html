Welcome to Jan20

Openings, tactics and endgames. Everything stays on this device.

  • Editor, Learn, Review Add lines, walk them, then review them on a spaced-repetition schedule. Drill, Time and Arena modes are in Learn.
  • Tactics Rated puzzles that adapt to you. Import the full Lichess set later in Settings.
  • Endgames Practise against Stockfish. Mistakes are taken back so you can try again.
